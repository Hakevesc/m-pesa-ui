FAQ Accordion .faq
What does it cost to send money?
Sending to another M-PESA user costs 1% of the amount, capped at ETB 50. Receiving money is always free.
Can I withdraw savings early?
Yes, but early withdrawal forfeits the current month's interest.
How do group payouts work?
Each round, the pooled contribution is paid to one member in the agreed rotation order.
