FAQ

WHAT IS TEREK BE M-PESA?

Terek Be M-PESA is a loyalty program that rewards you for your M-PESA transactions. Earn draw codes and stand a chance to win exciting prizes.

WHAT TYPES OF TRANSACTIONS MAKE ME ELIGIBLE FOR THE DRAW?

All M-PESA transactions including send money, buy goods, pay bill, and withdraw cash make you eligible for the draw.

WHAT ARE THE PRIZE TYPES?

We offer daily prizes, weekly prizes, and grand prizes. Prize amounts vary and are announced during the draw periods.

IF I WON THE DAILY PRIZE, AM I ELIGIBLE TO WIN THE OTHER PRIZES?

Yes! Winning a daily prize does not disqualify you from winning weekly or grand prizes. Keep transacting to earn more draw codes.

WHAT IS THE MINIMUM TRANSACTION I SHOULD DO TO EARN A DRAW CODE?

The minimum transaction amount to earn a draw code is KES 100. Each qualifying transaction earns you one draw code.

HOW MANY DRAW CODE CAN I EARN IN A DAY?

You can earn up to 10 draw codes per day. Each eligible transaction earns you one draw code.
